Fraunhofer Diffraction Through a Single Slit

When light passes through a very narrow slit, it spreads out instead of going straight. On a distant screen we see a wide bright central fringe with dimmer fringes on both sides, separated by dark fringes. Change the three sliders below to see how the pattern changes.

Calculation of dark fringes

Condition for dark fringes: a sin θ = mλ
For small angles: ym = m λ L / a

  • y = distance of the dark fringe from the centre of the screen
  • m = order of the dark fringe (1, 2, 3, ...)
  • λ = wavelength of light
  • L = distance between the slit and the screen
  • a = width of the slit

All values are converted to SI units (metres) before calculating.

Note: This is an educational visualization, not an exact experimental intensity measurement. The bright/dark pattern is drawn using a simple approximate intensity curve, and its brightness is boosted slightly so the side fringes are easy to see. The slit gap and the light source in the drawing are not to scale. The dark-fringe positions in the calculation section come directly from the formula.
